# AM Twilight Star Fix

<p class="lead">The best fix of the day — shoot 2–3 stars in the Stars tab while twilight still shows both stars and horizon.</p>

← Back to [Daily Fixes](/en/guides/days-work/)

---

## When

**Civil dawn → sunrise.** Watch the **Daily Schedule** in the Cel Nav Workbook — this step is tagged *optional* and the window closes fast (roughly 20–30 minutes).

Use **Solar Times** (and your position reference / AP) so the schedule times match your DR.

## In FairWinds (Guided)

1. Open the **Sky Tool** → **Workbook**
2. Confirm race + **Position reference** (or AP in Expert)
3. During the AM Twilight window, open the **Stars** tab
4. Tap **Sextant**, acquire a body, align to the horizon, **Mark!** → **Save**
5. Aim for **2–3 bodies**, altitude **15°–65°**
   - 2 stars → ~**90°** azimuth separation
   - 3 stars → ~**120°** spread for a triangle fix  
   See [Picking Stars for a Fix](star-selection/)
6. For each sight: tap **LOP** → **Reduce Sight →** → **Save LOP**
7. Check **2 or 3** entries under **Saved LOPs**
8. Tap **Compute Fix (Selected 2–3 LOPs)** → review fix quality → **Save Fix**

Optional: **Plot Selected LOPs** or **View Fix in 3D**. The saved fix shows up in the race viewer **Positions** panel (refresh if needed).

## Why it matters

A multi-star fix does not depend on a prior sun line or a long DR advance. If your DR has drifted, this is the observation that snaps you back before the day starts.

## Expert mode

Same sights, but there is no **LOP** / **Compute Fix** path. Follow [Expert setup](/en/guides/navigation-modes/#expert-setup): Correct Hs (IC + SD), reduce externally, then **Fixes** tab → **Enter Fix** (type **Star Fix**).
