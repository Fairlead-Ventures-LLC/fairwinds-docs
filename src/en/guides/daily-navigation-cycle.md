# The Daily Navigation Cycle

<p class="lead">Get the hang of the loop first. Detail comes later.</p>

← Back to [Celestial Navigation](/en/guides/celestial-navigation/)

---

Celestial navigation in FairWinds is the same short loop, repeated every day. You do not need to understand every abbreviation on a sight card to start — follow the cycle.

![The daily navigation cycle](/images/daily-navigation-cycle.png)

*The loop — then it starts again from your new fix.*

1. **Where am I?** — This is why you take fixes and make reckoning positions from them. At the start you know exactly where you are. But as you go along you use your last known good fix as a reference, or use a projected estimated position (called a dead reckoning position) as your starting point. Keeping track of where you are is the first starting point. 

   

2. **What time of day is it?** — In FairWinds, the sky tool is connected to your boat's position so you can visually check. But you can see what time of day it is above the solar table. Check the clock (UTC / ship time) and **Solar Times** in the Workbook — Sunrise, Sunset, Mer. Pass. Those times are computed from where you think you are. They are exactly what you would get from looking up in a celestial almanac. 

   ![solartimes](/images/solartimes.png)

   

3. **Next opportunity to use the sky** — There are many opportunities to use celestial objects throughout the day. Each type of 'fix' has a slightly different technique using sun, planets, stars. Open the **Daily Schedule**. It highlights the next useful window — twilight stars, sunrise lon, morning sun, noon, afternoon sun-run, sunset lon, evening stars. 

   The absolute easiest fix to do is to take a sun-run fix. Take a sight on the sun anytime in the morning. Then take another sight in the afternoon. Then use the worksheet to combine your sights into a fix after you make the second one. This is demonstrated in the video above.
   

4. **Taking the sight** — Open the **Sextant**, acquire the body, align it to the horizon, **Mark!** and **Save**. Sun work lives on the **Sun** tab; stars on the **Stars** tab.

   ![Taking a sight with the sextant](/images/image-20260813012812106.png)

5. **Converting to a fix** — Guided mode does the math: reduce LOPs and **Compute Fix** (stars), or use the **Sun Worksheet** (**Save to Log** for noon / lon / running fix). Expert mode: reduce externally, then **Enter Fix** on the Fixes tab. You can see this in the video above.

6. **Show on the map** — Saved fixes appear in **Positions** and on the chart. Build a new DR from that fix — that becomes “where am I?” for the next loop.

   ![refreshfix](/images/refreshfix.png)
