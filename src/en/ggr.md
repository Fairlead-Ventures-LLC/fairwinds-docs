# Virtual GGR - FAQ

How to join and race the Virtual Golden Globe Race on FairWinds.

![Virtual GGR course map](/images/ggr-world-map.png)

## Virtual GGR — quick tips on the race

1. The Virtual GGR follows the same course as the real sailors. There is 1) a full round-the-world and 2) a per-leg series for those who want to join along the way. Each new leg will be posted based on the real sailors' positions as they approach that section.

    To see the round-the-world course, click **Show course map** at the bottom of the [race detail page](https://fairwinds.world/race/2653/detail).

2. Everyone starts at the same time on Sept 6. To hide all the other boats, toggle off the **Boats** button in the tools menu. To choose your position on the start line just click the "A", "B", or "M" buttons. ![Screenshot 2026-08-29 at 11.49.50 AM](/images/Screenshot 2026-08-29 at 11.49.50 AM.png)

3. Your positions in the [public tracker](https://fairwinds.world/race/2653/tracker) are delayed 24 hours, so worst case you can't get too lost.

4. Waypoints are set up as wide gates. Cross through them in any direction to mark them as complete.
    ![Screenshot 2026-08-29 at 11.52.39 AM](/images/Screenshot 2026-08-29 at 11.52.39 AM.png)

5. No routers. No GPS browser hacking. Obvious violations will be cause for forced DNF.

6. Wave simulation is enabled and will impact your boat when they reach >3 meters. They can be viewed with the **Waves** button in the wind controls. See the [Waves](/en/guides/waves/) page for more detail on how they impact your boat. 

7. Important pages:

    - Global wind and wave patterns can be viewed in the FairWinds wind/map page at https://fairwinds.world/wind/map 
    - The celestial navigation sky viewer is at https://fairwinds.world/sky . Be sure to change your location so it is connected to the GGR race.

8. Ask for help! FairWinds has a dedicated multi-lingual **#golden-globe-2026** channel on the [Discord](https://discord.gg/qS3UVQzF6S) with many people willing to answer questions.
